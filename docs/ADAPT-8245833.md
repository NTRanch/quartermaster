# Adapt — the build-up drill (fathom RE 8245833) for Quartermaster

Design only; no game code changes, no new character generation
(D-292). Source: the fathom RE of Danbooru post 8245833
(`~/workspace/fathom-re-buildup-20261031/RECIPE.md`) — a 98.7 s
machine-restrained toy-play build-up whose measured structure is
a **staircase of held intensity floors** with one dipping
plateau and a single sustained peak. Register for this game, per
the commission: **measured training** — the build-up run as one
of Sloane's drills, against the clock and the count, scored the
way everything in Quartermaster is scored.

## What carries over

- **The staircase is the drill's round structure.** The drill
  machine (`src/drill.lua`) gains a build-up exercise shaped as
  eight held rounds, one per source segment: each round has a
  named intensity floor (the recipe's ×1.0 / 2.1 / 3.8 / 5.7 /
  8.8 / 7.7 / 17.0 / 12.6, carried as milli values in a data
  table, the way the eight positions live in `src/data.lua`).
  A round is a *hold at floor*: the trainee's task is steadiness
  at the called level for the round's full count, not climbing.
  Floors step at round boundaries only — mid-round escalation
  is a fault, exactly as breaking a called position is.
- **The plateau round is the drill's signature difficulty.**
  Round 6 calls a floor *below* round 5 (0.88×) while the
  expression requirement keeps rising — hold the body down,
  let the face climb. In drill terms: a hold whose two channels
  score in opposite directions, which the current phase machine
  (call → hold → appraise) expresses without new machinery.
- **The peak round is called once.** Round 7 is the only round
  at the peak floor, it is never repeated inside a sitting, and
  the sitting ends in the aftermath round at 0.74× — the drill
  has no cool-down round, because the source has none. A
  stopped sitting is witnessed and can never advance rank
  (the ceremony law in `src/ceremony.lua`, applied verbatim:
  the build-up sitting is a session record like any other).

## Mechanics mapping (onto the shipped systems)

- **Scoring:** the Appraisal Brush core mode already scores the
  guided work with stiffness presets; the build-up drill scores
  *floor-keeping* instead — deviation from the called floor
  accumulates as faults against the ceremony's hard ceiling of
  3, and the round ratio feeds `earns_step`'s per-rank gates
  (0.55 / 0.60 / 0.65 / 0.70) unchanged. A level can be cleared
  (pass bands 0.60 / 0.65 / 0.70) without the peak round earning
  the step — the teach → T1-gate arithmetic keeps its shape.
- **The count:** rounds are counted aloud in protocol form —
  the drill's existing call-and-hold cadence carries the count;
  the 0.25 s drive cycle of the source becomes the count's
  *tempo floor* at the peak rounds (four counts per second is
  the called cadence ceiling, named, not improvised).
- **The ledger:** every round appends to the evidence ledger
  (`src/ledger.lua`) as kind `buildup_round` with called floor,
  held ratio, and fault count — inside the existing whitelist /
  512-cap / audit discipline, so the Appraisal ceremony can
  read the build-up sitting as evidence beside the position
  sittings.
- **Persistence:** the sitting's best ratio and per-round bests
  join the `QM1|` record as new whitelisted keys under the
  strict reject-whole discipline; no format change for old keys.

## The Mala connection (design level, stated honestly)

Sloane's signature tool is the Mala (D-352): a worn instrument
whose Tide Chart is written per day, whose stations seal in
sequence, and whose law is *the day is the session*. The
build-up drill is the Mala's training-room mirror: the same
staircase the instrument runs across a wearing day, run here
in miniature, under appraisal, against the count. The drill
does not depict the Mala and adds no asset; it cites the
instrument's station sequence as the round order's rationale —
Root-first, one station per round's focus, the plateau round
at the station where the chart most often stalls. That is the
whole connection, and it is enough: drill and instrument teach
the same body the same staircase in two registers.

## Face states

The source's ladder — composed → absorbed → strained-vocal →
tearful → climax — maps onto the drill's appraisal surface as
the *expected* face per round, checked at appraisal like a
position: rounds 1–2 composed, 3–4 absorbed, 5 strained, 6
tearful (the plateau round's rising channel), 7 the peak face,
8 aftermath. States interpolate (ramps, never snaps — the W4
law this program already keeps); a face a full ladder-step
ahead of its round reads as anticipation and is scored as a
fault of discipline, not of feeling.

## Divergences from the source, plainly

- The source's peak window is 8.1 s of a 98.7 s take; the
  drill's peak round runs on the drill's own round clock, not
  the source's proportions — the *ratios between floors* are
  what ported, not the durations.
- The source has no daze and no taper; the drill likewise ends
  the sitting in aftermath and lets the ceremony, not a
  cool-down, close the record.
- The restraint apparatus and the recording overlay are the
  source's production design; nothing of them enters the game.
- The source is solo; so is the drill. No partner is added.

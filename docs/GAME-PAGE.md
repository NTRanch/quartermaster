## What you do

A position is called — by name, by number, or, in the first drill, with a ghost of the pose. Answer on the position pads before the bar runs out; a wrong pad is a mark against the call, and three lose it. Then hold: the figure sways, wider as the hold goes on, and you brace to steady her. Bracing drains poise, and poise only returns while you are not bracing. Some calls are recalls: a sequence of positions is shown, then asked back from memory, one pad at a time.

Composure is three marks — timeouts and lost calls cost one each, and losing all three ends the drill in failure. Clearing a drill unlocks the next. At the ceremony the whole ledger is shown — every call, every score — before it rules, and rank climbs one step per qualifying ceremony.

## Features

- Three drills: First Drill (positions called by name, with a ghost of the pose), Second Drill (calls by name and number, with recalls from memory), and Third Drill (calls by number alone, two recall rounds, a tighter scoring brush)
- An evidence ledger and appraisal ceremony: scoring is reaction speed and steadiness, fifty points each, scaled by the brush stiffness preset (soft / firm / severe)
- Readable Lua source running in the page — view source to read the game — with progress persisted in the browser's local storage

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Answer a called position | Click, tap, or keys 1–8 |
| Brace and steady the hold | Hold the pad, or hold SPACE |
| Pause | ESC, or the Pause button |
| Stop the drill at once, without penalty | The red Stop button |
